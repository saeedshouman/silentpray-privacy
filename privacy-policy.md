# Privacy Policy

**Silent Prayer App** takes your privacy seriously. To better protect your privacy we provide this privacy policy notice explaining the way your personal information is collected and used.

**Effective Date:** November 29, 2025

---

## Collection of Routine Information

This app does not track any personal information about users. The app only collects location data locally on your device to calculate prayer times. This information is not transmitted to any external servers and remains on your device only.

---

## Permissions

The app requires the following permissions:

- **Location Access:** Required to calculate accurate prayer times based on your geographical location. This data stays on your device.
- **Notifications:** To send prayer time reminders and alerts.
- **Do Not Disturb Access:** To automatically enable silent mode during prayer times.
- **Internet Access:** Optional, used only for prayer time calculations. No personal data is transmitted.
- **Boot Completed:** To ensure notifications work after device restart.

---

## Data Storage

All your settings, preferences, and prayer times are stored **locally on your device**. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to external servers.

---

## Cookies

This app does not use cookies.

---

## Advertisement and Third Parties

This app does not display advertisements and does not use any third-party tracking or analytics services. We do not share any data with third parties.

---

## Security

The security of your personal information is important to us. Since all data is stored locally on your device and we do not collect or transmit any personal information, your privacy is protected by default.

---

## Children's Privacy

This app does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13 years of age. The app is suitable for all ages.

---

## Changes To This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy is effective as of November 29, 2025 and will remain in effect except with respect to any changes in its provisions in the future, which will be in effect immediately after being posted on this page.

We reserve the right to update or change our Privacy Policy at any time. If we make any material changes to this Privacy Policy, we will notify you by updating the date at the top of this policy.
